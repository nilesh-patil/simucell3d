About SimuCell3D#

SimuCell3D was created by Steve Runser in the Iber Lab (ETH Zurich). It was developed in the group of Dagmar Iber at the Department of Biosystems Science and Engineering (D-BSSE) of ETH Zurich and the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics (SIB) in Basel. The article lists the author contributions as: concept, Roman Vetter and Dagmar Iber; model and algorithm development, Steve Runser and Roman Vetter; implementation, numerical simulations and figures, Steve Runser. The software and its applications are described in Runser, Vetter & Iber (2024), Nature Computational Science (Citing SimuCell3D). The project was called DCM before release 2.0.

Releases#

Release 1.0 is the code published with the article, distributed from the Iber Lab repository. Two snapshots of it exist, and they differ:

  • The Zenodo archive cited by the article, doi:10.5281/zenodo.10796908 (published 2024-03-08), contains commit 5342bf8. Its default contact model is 1 (node-node coupling).

  • The tag v1.0 of this repository is commit 38af451 (2024-06-26), a later commit by the same author and the head of the Iber Lab repository. It adds the input meshes and parameter files of Figs. 1 and 3 and makes contact model 2 (face-face coupling) the default. The source files in src/ are the same as in the archive.

Which contact model produced each published figure is not documented (Release 1.0).

Releases 2.0 and 2.1 are maintenance releases of SimuCell3D, developed by Nilesh Patil at nilesh-patil/simucell3d. They keep the published model but change its implementation (bug fixes, bending-force guards), add safeguards that remove cells by criteria that are not part of the paper, and improve parallel performance. Because these changes alter simulation results, the published figures should be compared with release 1.0 (Release history). The 2.x releases have not been reviewed or endorsed by the authors of the article, the Iber Lab or ETH Zurich.
