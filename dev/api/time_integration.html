Time integration#

time_integration_scheme moves the nodes at the end of each iteration from the forces accumulated on them. DYNAMIC_MODEL_INDEX selects the equations at compile time: 0 solves the full equations of motion with the semi-implicit Euler scheme, 1 solves the overdamped equations with the forward Euler scheme. With contact models 1 and 2, coupled nodes on neighbouring cells are moved together. The narrative description is in Time integration.

Integrator#

class time_integration_scheme#

Moves the nodes of all cells from the forces accumulated on them, one time step at a time.

The equations are selected at compile time by DYNAMIC_MODEL_INDEX (global_configuration.hpp):

  • 0: full equations of motion, semi-implicit Euler scheme: the momentum p of a node of mass m is updated first, p += (F - (zeta/m) p) dt, then the position, x += (p/m) dt;

  • 1: overdamped equations of motion, forward Euler scheme: x += (F/zeta) dt.

Here zeta is the damping coefficient (kg/s) and dt the time step (s). Static cells are not moved. With CONTACT_MODEL_INDEX 1 or 2, nodes coupled across a cell-cell contact are moved together with their averaged force (and momentum). The forces of the nodes are reset after the update.
