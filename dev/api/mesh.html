Cell meshes#

A cell is a closed, oriented triangulated surface made of node, edge and face objects. The cell computes its geometry (volume, area, curvature) and the mechanical forces of its own membrane: pressure, surface tension, area elasticity and bending (Cell mechanics). Nodes carry position, force and, with the full equations of motion, momentum. Faces carry their face type and, with contact models 1 and 2, nodes store their coupling partners on neighbouring cells. The five cell classes derived from cell are documented in Cell classes.

Cell#

class cell : public std::enable_shared_from_this<cell>#

A cell: a closed, oriented, triangulated surface with its own mechanics.

The cell owns its nodes and faces (stored in vectors whose free slots are reused) and the set of its edges. It computes its geometry (area, volume, centroid, face normals, node curvature) and, in apply_internal_forces(), the forces of its own membrane:

  • pressure p = -K ln(V/V0), capped at the cell type’s maximum pressure, applied along the face normals (K bulk modulus, V0 target volume);

  • surface tension, from the surface tension of each face type;

  • area elasticity with energy (ka/2)(A/A0 - 1)^2, where the target area A0 = (r V^2)^(1/3) follows from the target isoperimetric ratio r = A^3/V^2;

  • bending, from the bending modulus of each face type;

  • a regularization force that keeps triangle angles near 60 degrees.

The target volume grows at the cell’s growth rate each time step. Growth rate and division volume are drawn once per cell from normal distributions (clipped to 3 standard deviations) given by the cell type.

Derived classes (epithelial_cell, ecm_cell, lumen_cell, nucleus_cell, static_cell) override division, polarization and motion; see Cell classes.

Subclassed by ecm_cell, epithelial_cell, lumen_cell, nucleus_cell, static_cell

Contact energies

Sums of the adhesion and repulsion energies of the faces, J.

Note

Present only when FACE_STORE_CONTACT_ENERGY is true.

void initialize_cell_properties(bool check_cell_integrity = true) noexcept(false)#

Prepare a newly created cell for the simulation.

Sets the local ids of nodes and faces, frees unused nodes and, if check_cell_integrity is true, builds the edge set, checks that the surface is a closed manifold and orients the faces outward. Then computes face normals and areas, the cell area and volume, sets the target area and target volume to the current values and, if the cell has a cell type, draws its growth rate and division volume.

Throws:

  • mesh_integrity_exception – If an edge is shared by more than two faces.

  • initial_triangulation_exception – If the surface is not a closed manifold.

void initialize_random_properties() noexcept#

Draw the growth rate (m^3/s) and division volume (m^3) of the cell.

Each is drawn from a normal distribution with the mean and standard deviation of the cell type and clipped to the mean plus or minus 3 standard deviations; with a standard deviation of 0 (or an infinite mean division volume) the mean is used.

virtual void apply_internal_forces(const double time_step) noexcept#

Update the geometry of the cell and add its membrane forces to its nodes.

stability_report check_numerical_stability(const double max_extent_ratio = 10., const double aabb_volume_tolerance = 1e-3) const noexcept#

Check whether the mesh of the cell has diverged.

A cell is unstable if a node has a non-finite coordinate, or if its AABB is wider than max_extent_ratio times the cube root of its target volume (a healthy cell of target volume V0 spans about 1.2 V0^(1/3)). A non-static cell is also unstable if its surface is inside out (signed volume <= 0) or intersects itself (|signed volume| larger than the AABB volume times 1 + aabb_volume_tolerance; a sphere fills pi/6 of its AABB). The signed volume is the one of the last volume update. The solver removes unstable cells (a 2.1 safeguard).

Parameters:

  • max_extent_ratio – Maximum AABB extent in units of V0^(1/3), dimensionless.

  • aabb_volume_tolerance – Relative tolerance of the AABB volume test, dimensionless.

Nodes, edges and faces#

class node#

Node (vertex) of a cell surface mesh.

A node stores its position (m) and the force acting on it (N). With the full equations of motion (DYNAMIC_MODEL_INDEX 0) it also stores its momentum (kg m/s). With the coupling contact models (CONTACT_MODEL_INDEX 1 or 2) it also stores its normal, its curvature and its coupling partners on neighbouring cells, protected by a per-node std::mutex.

Nodes are owned by their cell and identified by their index in the cell’s node list (the local id). A node whose slot is free has is_used() false.

Note

Members inside #if CONTACT_MODEL_INDEX or #if DYNAMIC_MODEL_INDEX blocks exist only for those values of the macros in global_configuration.hpp.

class edge#

Edge of a cell surface mesh, defined by two node ids and up to two face ids.

The node ids are stored in increasing order, so that an edge does not depend on the direction in which it was created. All ids are local to the owning cell. An edge with two faces is manifold (see is_manifold()).

class face#

Triangular face of a cell surface mesh.

A face stores the local ids of its three nodes, its unit outward normal, its area (m^2) and its face type, given as the index of the face type in the cell type’s list of face types. Normals and areas are computed by the owning cell (cell::update_all_face_normals_and_areas()). Faces live in the face list of their cell; a face whose slot is free has is_used() false.

Node ids

Local ids of the first, second and third node (the face must be used).

Contact energies

Adhesion and repulsion energies accumulated on the face, J.

Note

Present only when FACE_STORE_CONTACT_ENERGY is true.
