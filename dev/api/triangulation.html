Triangulation and division#

These classes create and maintain the cell surface meshes. local_mesh_refiner keeps edge lengths between the minimum edge length and three times that value by splitting, collapsing and swapping edges at every iteration (Mesh refinement). cell_divider splits a cell that has reached its division volume along the plane normal to its longest axis (Growth, division and removal). When perform_initial_triangulation is enabled, initial_triangulation re-triangulates the input meshes with poisson_sampling and ball_pivoting_algorithm.

Mesh refinement#

class local_mesh_refiner#

Keeps the edge lengths of every cell surface in [l_min, l_max].

Called by the solver at every iteration. An edge longer than l_max is split at its midpoint; an edge shorter than l_min is collapsed into a node at its midpoint, unless the collapse would make an edge shared by more than two faces. If edge swaps are enabled, triangles whose quality score (36/sqrt(3) * area / perimeter^2, 1 for an equilateral triangle) is below 0.2 first have their longest edge swapped. With the full equations of motion (DYNAMIC_MODEL_INDEX 0), the momentum of the nodes is redistributed so that it is conserved by each operation.

The solver uses l_max = 3 * l_min, with l_min the min_edge_length of the parameter file.

void refine_mesh(cell_ptr c) const noexcept(false)#

Refine the mesh of one cell.

Swaps edges of low-quality triangles (if enabled), then splits and collapses edges until every edge is in [l_min, l_max]. If more than 50 split or collapse operations fall in the same cube of side 1e-7 m (a split/collapse cycle), the refinement stops early for this call, leaving some edges out of range, and a line is written to standard error (the first 20 occurrences, then every 1000th). Finally the face winding is checked and repaired (repair_inconsistent_winding()).

Parameters:

c – The cell.

Throws:

mesh_integrity_exception – if 500000 operations are reached without convergence.

static uint64_t winding_checksum(const cell_ptr &c) noexcept#

Fast check of the face winding in one pass over the faces.

Cell division#

class cell_divider#

Divides cells that have reached their division volume.

All methods are static; the class cannot be instantiated. The solver calls run() every 5 iterations. A cell is cut by the plane through its centroid normal to its longest axis (cell::get_cell_division_axis()). The cut interface is triangulated with Poisson-disk sampling and a 2D Delaunay triangulation, each daughter receives one side of the surface plus the interface, and both daughters are refined by the local mesh refiner.

A division can be refused. The mother cell is then left unchanged, a line “Cell division failed for cell <id>: <reason>” is written to standard error, and the division is tried again at the next check. Reasons include: no closed cut curve can be traced; the Delaunay triangulation does not cover every side of the interface outline; the cut does not split the surface into exactly two pieces. When the plane meets the surface along several closed curves, the cell is cut along the longest one.

Initial triangulation#

class initial_triangulation#

Re-triangulates a closed input surface; all methods are static.

Used by simulation_initializer when perform_initial_triangulation is enabled. The procedure is:

  1. Coarsely triangulate the surface by connecting the edges of each non-triangular face to the face centre.

  2. Convert it to a cell, which orients the faces outward and checks that the surface is closed and manifold.

  3. Sample the surface with Poisson-disk sampling (points at least l_min apart).

  4. Reconstruct a triangulated surface from the points with the ball pivoting algorithm.

The edge lengths of the result are brought into [l_min, l_max] later by the local mesh refiner.

class poisson_sampling#

Poisson-disk sampling of a triangulated surface; all methods are static.

Returns points on the surface that are at least l_min apart. The surface is first sampled densely and uniformly; points are then accepted voxel by voxel in a uniform spatial partitioning grid if they are at least l_min from the points already accepted. The method follows Bowers et al., “Parallel Poisson disk sampling with spectrum analysis on surfaces”, SIGGRAPH Asia 2010.

class ball_pivoting_algorithm#

Reconstructs a closed triangulated surface from a Poisson-disk point cloud.

Implements the ball pivoting algorithm of Bernardini et al., “The ball-pivoting algorithm

for surface reconstruction”, IEEE TVCG 5(4), 1999, doi:10.1109/2945.817351, with a ball of radius 1.7 * l_min. After the front has been expanded, holes of up to about 10 nodes are closed by fans around a new centre node. Used by

initial_triangulation.
